Requirements modeling · 2-person team

ScheduleEase

2023

The question"What do I still need to graduate?" Penn State's CS and SE degree requirements are nested and full of exceptions, and students lose track of them.

Actually neededAn answer students can trust, checked against every rule and built from records they already have.

What I didRules too tangled to check by hand. I modeled the requirements as structured data that a rule-matching engine can check a student's record against.

No source for completed courses. There was no API to pull them, so I used the unofficial transcript students can export from the school portal, and wrote a parser that gets clean course records out of its malformed HTML.

ResultCaught real discrepancies before they could mislead a student, such as honors sections not being counted toward the base course they satisfy.

  • Java
  • Spring Boot
  • Rule engine
  • HTML parsing

Measuring Student Engagement

2023 – 2024

The questionResearchers wanted to measure how engaged students are with course material. Teammates built the eye tracking and facial-expression analysis.

Actually neededOne tool that runs a whole session, from consent to the final quiz, with those modules recording alongside, and never crashes partway.

What I didRandom crashes, no clear pattern. The lab computer has one webcam, but each part of the app opened its own connection to it, and now and then they collided. I made the app open the camera once and share it, which ended the crashes.

Every participant, the same steps. I built the controller that runs the session in order, with every study material in its own folder of readable YAML files.

ResultRan real sessions with 17 participants; the team validated their models against the dataset.

  • Python
  • PyQt5
  • OpenCV

Side Projects

AI pipeline & motion design · For a friend

Animating a Music Video

2026

The questionA friend needed a 40-second animated sequence for her music video, starting from one sketch-style drawing of a singer.

Actually neededArms reaching toward the viewer and bending sharply, which After Effects can't fake; it has to be drawn frame by frame.

What I didAsked only for the layers the motion needed. I separated the rest myself in After Effects.

Split the work by strength. She designed the moves and drew the key poses; I directed Claude in building a free, local ComfyUI workflow to generate the frames in between.

Held the AI frames to her drawings. I reviewed every sequence to keep her hand-drawn style, and suggested generating in reverse when line art dropped out.

Result14 animated sequences. After two long sessions refining the workflow, nine sequences took about three hours.

  • After Effects
  • ComfyUI
  • Claude
